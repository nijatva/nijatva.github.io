What it does

Shipped features

  • Library from MediaStore, with a folders view
  • Keeps playing in the background, with lock-screen controls
  • Equaliser with presets, bass boost and a sleep timer
  • Runs in the browser too, no install
  • No recommendations, no listening history

Like every app in the suite: no Google Play Services, no account, no ads, no analytics. Distribution is an APK, never a store.
