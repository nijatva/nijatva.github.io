Two ways to use it

Same idea, two engines

Pick whichever fits the moment.

Android app

The whole camera: manual ISO, shutter and focus, RAW and DNG, night and astro stacking, Auto HDR, panorama, looks and sliders. Works with no network at all.

Web app

What it does

Everything a photo needs

Nothing on this list needs an account, a server or a Play Services library.

Pro controls

Manual ISO, shutter time and focus behind CONTROL_AE_MODE_OFF, with a live readout of what the sensor is actually doing.

RAW and DNG

RAW capture written as a DNG, and a 10-bit packed path where the sensor allows it - half the file, same information.

Night and astro stacking

Pyramid alignment and an inverse-variance merge in our own C++, then demosaic, white balance and a tone curve that puts 18% grey where it belongs.

Auto HDR

When the frame is clipping, a plain photo quietly becomes a short burst and is merged, so the sky keeps its colour instead of turning white.

Grading after the shot

Five looks, per-lens tuning, four sliders and an optional portrait blur. The same pipeline every time, so a look can never break a capture.

Framing aids

Grid, horizon level with a haptic tick, focus peaking and a histogram - and the photo keeps the exposure and white balance the preview showed you.

Part of a suite

Hands shots to Nijatva Gallery and Nijatva Photos, and accepts IMAGE_CAPTURE and VIDEO_CAPTURE, so any other app can ask it for a photo.

How it is built

Camera2, and our own maths

  • Camera2 API directly. No CameraX, no Play Services, no analytics, no ads
  • Two permissions in total: CAMERA and RECORD_AUDIO. No INTERNET, so nothing can leave the phone even by accident
  • Night, astro and HDR merge run in C++: pyramid alignment, inverse-variance merge, demosaic, white balance, colour matrix, tone curve
  • Grading is Kotlin, after the capture: looks, per-lens tuning, four sliders, chroma denoise, thresholded sharpening, optional portrait blur
  • The preview and the photo agree: the capture locks the preview's exposure and white balance, and the framing maths match what the viewfinder showed

Build it from a plain checkout - JDK 17, SDK 35, NDK 27.2.12479018 and CMake 3.22.1, all pinned by the native module.

git clone https://github.com/nijatva/nijatva-camera
cd nijatva-camera
./gradlew :app:assembleFullDebug
